\begin{abstract}
Sensor faults can resemble environmental changes, while sparse acquisition can conceal short events. We evaluate a bounded single-node integration of existing health diagnostics, event-aware schedules and snapshot emission as a simulation-based systems study. The original mechanism (G1) compares frozen internal modes with six fixed periods, independent delta/variance schedules and a clearly labeled Rao-inspired control, using ten development and twenty held-out seeds over 45 scenarios. Native behavior and shared-detector schedule isolation are distinguished. A separately specified stochastic generator (G2) transfers 14 selected systems without retuning to twenty new seeds and 46 scenarios, adding correlated noise, variable-duration events, independent event/fault timing and both-channel faults. In G1, SU40 achieved F1 \SUFone{} and Recall \SURecall{}; its near-budget advantage over Variance was conditional. \input{tables/transfer_abstract.tex} All communication counts use actual stateless Binary v1 encoding and independent receiver decoding. Lower transmission counts and fault-induced reports coexist with missed events and increased ordinary-data age; neither a universally superior mode nor equal-budget quality superiority is established. Two project-defined synthetic mechanisms and ideal local delivery provide stress evidence, without natural-fault, field-effectiveness or measured-energy validation.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Resource-constrained agricultural monitoring must allocate acquisition and communication effort while retaining useful environmental changes. A sensor may report an out-of-range value, become stuck, drift, or disappear. Responding to every apparent change can waste resources and produce environmental false reports; suppressing questionable readings can discard real events. Sampling and emission introduce distinct losses: an unobserved event cannot be recovered from a correct transmitter, and an observed state transition may remain absent from receiver history if it is not emitted.

Adaptive sampling, fault-aware event detection, predictive transmission, and their coordination have substantial precedents \citep{rao2019adaptive,masoum2012quality,yim2010fault,dias2017dual,arnaiz2024energy}. We therefore ask a narrower empirical question: across two independently specified two-channel simulation mechanisms, what detection, acquisition, communication, and information-age tradeoffs result from integrating existing health diagnostics with causal sampling and explicit emission rules? Are apparent gains retained against independent schedules at nearby acquisition budgets, and which conclusions survive a change of signal, noise, event and fault distributions without retuning?

The contribution is threefold. First, we expose an executable integration with full business fields, missing-value semantics, and independently decoded receiver edges. Second, we compare frozen internal modes with independent fixed, delta, variance, and literature-inspired schedules under both native and shared-detector views, retaining adverse operating points. Third, we transfer frozen systems without recalibration to a separately specified stochastic generator with both-channel faults and independently timed variable-duration events, and provide seed-level paired uncertainty, budget requalification, counterfactual fault attribution, and an explicit account of observability and modeling limits. These are systems-evaluation contributions. We do not claim first invention of trust-aware sensing, joint sampling and communication, health diagnostics, deadband transmission, or binary compression.

\section{Related Work}
\paragraph{Adaptive and event-driven acquisition.}
Rao et al. combine compressed sensing with prediction-based acquisition and adapt switching around critical regions \citep{rao2019adaptive}. Masoum et al. exploit correlation and predictions in decentralized quality-aware sampling \citep{masoum2012quality}. DSRA changes acquisition according to signal changes within an adaptive software architecture \citep{algabroun2020dsra}. These establish both adaptation and architectural integration as prior work. Our Delta and Variance controls are simple independently implemented engineering samplers; they are not reproductions of those papers.

\paragraph{Faults and environmental events.}
Sharma et al. compare fault-detection families on real deployment datasets \citep{sharma2010faults}; this evidential scope is stronger than injected-fault simulation. Yim and Choi use neighboring readings and confidence to adapt fault-tolerant event decisions \citep{yim2010fault}. CLARA uses cluster correlation for sampling and reconstructs missing readings at the sink \citep{harb2024clara}. TAIS has no spatial redundancy or reconstruction of erased environmental truth. Its SensorTrust C11 health rules are an unchanged separately pinned dependency.

\paragraph{Communication and coordinated control.}
Dias et al. analyze dual prediction and aggregation \citep{dias2017dual}. Arnaiz et al. coordinate dynamic sampling and dual prediction under energy constraints \citep{arnaiz2024energy}. Thus coordinated acquisition and communication, multi-mode tradeoffs, and resource objectives are not new concepts here. TAIS emits complete last-sent-deadband snapshots with heartbeat and mandatory state edges. It has no synchronized predictor, network-level optimization, or demonstrated hard energy guarantee. The repository's related-work matrix distinguishes inspected abstracts from detailed method inspection; no cross-paper performance numbers are treated as directly comparable benchmark results.

SensorTrust C11 supplies per-channel state, score, and flags. These are heuristic diagnostics, not calibrated fault probabilities. AdaptiveSense provides the original change analyzer and scheduler. GateOnly uses causal health gating and recovery reseeding without the later confirmation mode. JO-S adds bounded rechecks. JO-SU-20/30/40 retain that sampling design with different idle caps and attach U. U compares current sampled values with the last sent snapshot, emits on event or health state/flag edges and validity changes, applies a two-noise-floor deadband, and has a 120-second heartbeat evaluated at sample times. Health score is retained in every emitted snapshot even when score-only changes do not trigger emission.

Binary v1 preserves timestamps, two values including null, two event booleans, and all available health state/score/flags. We measure encoded byte length, excluding hexadecimal logging representation. Every frame is stateless and independently decodable; CRC checks accidental corruption and provides no authentication. Layout provisioning is shared configuration. There is no ACK, retransmission, packet fragmentation, or deployed LoRaWAN/MQTT stack. The evaluated link delivers every emitted complete frame locally. The receiver only sees decoded frames and detects event starts from received false-to-true transitions. Local cleared states are not implicitly transmitted.

The historical H layer added trend-based health suspicion but lost true events and increased other false reports. It is excluded from the present frozen SU modes; its negative findings remain in the historical evidence rather than being presented as a new successful contribution. GateOnly remains the historical default; this paper does not retroactively reverse prior NO-GO decisions.

\section{Methodology}
\subsection{Synthetic data and split}
Each run contains 1,800 one-second source rows with temperature and humidity, a smooth low-amplitude background, independent Gaussian observation noise, and trapezoidal environmental interventions. The generator separates latent environmental values, noisy pre-fault observations, and faulted observations. Labels use physical intervention definitions rather than a method's detection score. Three clean controls and seven fault classes (range, spike, stuck, drift, missing, invalid, stable offset), each crossed with no event, same-channel event, or cross-channel event and short/long profiles, yield 45 scenarios. The actual labeled event window can differ from the injected trapezoid support. In G1, faults affect temperature; cross-channel events affect humidity. This asymmetry is retained in the original results and is separately stress-tested by G2, rather than retrospectively changing G1.

We audited accessible local structured artifacts, Git-ref histories, and archive seed identities before allocating development seeds 600--609 and final seeds 610--629. Seeds 0--599 were reserved. Fresh seeds change noise and timing jitter within the same generator; they are not external data generalization. All methods share the identical observed trace for each seed/scenario, verified by fingerprints. No external greenhouse record is included in the effectiveness experiment; unresolved source-clock semantics from prior work are not repaired by interpolation.

\subsection{Independent transfer generator and zero-retuning protocol}
G2 is specified in a separate module and configuration; it does not call G1 generation or use detector output to accept instances. The background is a discrete mean-reverting process,
\begin{equation}
b_{c,t}=b_{c,t-1}+0.003(\mu_{c,t}-b_{c,t-1})+s_c z_{c,t},
\end{equation}
with piecewise random targets lasting 180--360s. Centers are uniform 22--28$^\circ$C and 40--60\%RH; target standard deviations are0.35$^\circ$C/1.75\%RH and innovation scales 0.006$^\circ$C/0.03\%RH. Temperature/humidity innovation correlation changes between segments within $[-0.65,0.2]$. Separate observation noise is AR(1) with channel-specific coefficient 0.65--0.9, base scales 0.08$^\circ$C/0.4\%RH, and amplitude multipliers 0.6--1.8 changing every 120 s. With probability 0.002, an innovation adds a scaled Student-$t_3$ perturbation. These are declared stress distributions, not fitted agricultural estimates.

Each event-enabled channel receives one short 12--40s, medium 41--100s and long 101--240s intervention, permuted among independently sampled onset strata 180--300,660--780 and 1140--1260s. Step/exponential-recovery, smooth sine-squared and asymmetric beta-shaped pulses replace G1's fixed trapezoid. Signed magnitudes are1.8--4.8$^\circ$C/6--18\%RH. G2 truth is the complete exogenous support $[t_0,t_0+d)$, represented by inclusive scoring window $[t_0,t_0+d-1]$ on the 1 Hz grid; it is never thresholded using ChangeAnalyzer. This differs from G1's physical-amplitude-derived interior labels. Consequently, absolute G1/G2 rates describe different tasks; we compare within-generator contrasts and direction stability, not pooled superiority.

All seven fault kinds occur on either channel. Fault starts are independently drawn from 150--1300s, core durations 120--360s. Range replacements are explicitly outside physical limits; missing and invalid mean None and NaN; stuck holds the actual onset reading. Spikes comprise three independently placed 1--3s bursts, scored as separate episodes. Signed offsets and three-segment random drift slopes have 30--100s linear recovery tails. The 46-case matrix contains four clean controls and seven kinds $\times$ two fault channels $\times$ no/same/cross event arrangements. Event-enabled fault runs contain three events; the clean both-channel control contains six. Before/after/overlap/nonoverlap relations are classified from independent schedules, not prescribed by a fault-relative delay. All difficult and fully overwritten events remain in the denominators.

Independent SHA256-derived RNG streams generate environment, noise, event family and fault channel. Fault-kind comparisons share the exact background/noise/event stream when intended. Counterfactuals copy the same noisy physical sequence and remove only faults; cache identity hashes the complete clean observations and labels, preventing old relation/profile cache collisions. Online replay receives only an observation facade, without scenario, seed, labels, latent/noisy arrays or hidden parameters. Unsampled-value replacement and future-truncation tests verify causal decisions.

Engineering seeds 690/691 validate software only. The specification, source/analysis hashes, scenarios, metrics and unchanged operating points were committed before the single formal 20-seed campaign (700--719), yielding 12,880 native-system runs across 14 methods. The five frozen TAIS modes, six fixed +C11 periods and three frozen SU40-targeted independent+C11 controls are transferred without selection or tuning. In particular Rao-inspired retains 24$^\circ$C/45\%RH priors despite G2's different hidden centers. A favorable or adverse result does not trigger recalibration. G2 has its own 5,000-draw whole-seed paired bootstrap (RNG seed 2026101923), with no G1/G2 pooling. Actual G2 budget gaps independently determine the original $\pm5\%$ qualification. Per-event coverage/duration/masking and raw any-flag versus correct-type diagnostics supplement the unchanged matcher; stable offset has no C11 type bit and typed detection is N/A.

\subsection{Independent schedules and explicit shared components}
Fixed5/10/20/30/40/60 reuse the frozen replay implementation, sample the first available source row at or after due time, and upload every sample. On the dense integer grid this gives strict periodic times. They read only due-time values and have no native health diagnostic. The common event analyzer sees only actual sampled points.

For Delta, with channel noise scale $\sigma_c$, consecutive finite observations define
\begin{equation}
D_t=\max_c\left\{\frac{|x_{c,t}-x_{c,t^-}|}{\sigma_c},\;\frac{20|x_{c,t}-x_{c,t^-}|}{\sigma_c(t-t^-)}\right\}.
\end{equation}
Crossing a development-selected threshold requests the minimum interval. Variance uses elapsed-time EWMA statistics, with $\alpha=1-\exp[-(t-t^-)/60]$, residual $d=x_t-m_{t^-}$, $m_t=m_{t^-}+\alpha d$, and $v_t=(1-\alpha)(v_{t^-}+\alpha d^2)$. It triggers on $\max_c\sqrt{v_{c,t}}/\sigma_c$. Both restore the interval by doubling after 60 seconds of sampled stability, up to the cap. They use constant bounded state; invalidity transitions trigger a dense period without classifying a physical fault.

Rao-inspired uses at most four sampled timestamp/value pairs per channel for least-squares trend fitting and predicts entry into a prior critical band. This is inspired by Rao's line fitting and boundary-time reasoning (their Eqs.~11--13), but is not their full algorithm. Bands are centered on public configured priors (24$^\circ$C,45\%RH), with width set by selected noise-floor multiples. A sampled excursion or a predicted crossing within one cap requests dense observations; an inner 75\% band and stable dwell govern return. There is no CS reconstruction, previous-day Student-$t$ tolerance, dual-end prediction, model training/synchronization, or prediction-based substitution. The original paper's performance and guarantees cannot be transferred to this adaptation.

All three native independent samplers use the same sampled-only ChangeAnalyzer for event decisions and upload every sample. Explicit ``+C11'' versions add unchanged diagnostics without affecting schedules/detection. Three cap 40 ``+C11+U'' versions add the same TAIS emission component as a diagnostic ablation and are not completely independent original systems. The \emph{common} view applies identical per-channel ChangeAnalyzer, metadata-only C11, and every-sample emission to every native schedule, including TAIS. It isolates the consequences of sample times, not a complete independent-system comparison. Native TAIS sampling decisions still originate from its native internal health and event states.

\subsection{Development selection and budget matching}
At cap 40, thresholds 2/4/8 for Delta and Variance and 4/8/12 for Rao-inspired are ranked by development micro F1, then Recall, fewer samples, and smaller threshold. Selected thresholds are \DeltaThreshold, \VarianceThreshold, and \RaoThreshold, respectively. Native minimum 5~s and caps 20/40/60 remain in the final tradeoff curves. All fixed periods remain.

An initial cap-only development search failed to match either target; those results are retained. A disclosed development-only refinement searches independent minimum intervals 10/15/20/25/30/35s and caps 20--60~s in 5~s steps, requiring minimum $\leq$cap, at the already selected quality thresholds. Among points within 5\% of the GateOnly or SU40 development sample total, the rule selects highest F1, then closest budget, larger cap, and smaller minimum; if none qualify, it retains the closest failure. These bounds impose explicit acquisition budgets and can reduce short-event observability. Frozen TAIS points are unchanged. Actual final gaps determine whether a comparison qualifies as approximately matched; final data never adjust parameters. All code/configuration/points were committed before final execution.

\subsection{Outcomes and uncertainty}
The frozen one-to-one channel-aware event matcher uses a 5~s tolerance. A report may therefore precede a label boundary by up to 5~s; this tolerance does not imply forecasting or future access. We report TP/FP/FN and derived Precision/Recall/F1 separately for sender-on-upload and receiver-edge reports, with local detection counts also retained. Recall is undefined, not zero, in no-event runs. Short/long and same/cross groups remain separate. Conditional event delays must be read together with misses; matched-in-both delay differences are separately available. The operational ``fault-induced'' count is an unmatched fault-channel report within the fault or 120~s recovery window that lacks a coincident report (within 5~s) in the same-policy clean counterfactual. This is a simulator attribution definition, not proof of physical causal diagnosis.

Costs count actual sampled cycles, uploads, encoded bytes, mean bytes/upload, source-second pooled P95/maximum age, and sample-to-emission delay for exact sampled event/health signatures. Physical-event-to-report delay is distinct. No receiver carries a future value backward in time, and decoded null remains null. Health detection uses raw C11 flags; capability and rate are N/A for methods without health metadata.

We resample whole random seeds in paired fashion (5,000 bootstrap draws, fixed RNG2026101917); scenario rows and source seconds within a seed are not independent replicates. Intervals describe uncertainty within the synthetic family, and many contrasts/endpoints prohibit a broad significance claim from one favorable comparison. All per-run metrics, grouped summaries, candidate results, fingerprints, and frame archives are retained or deterministically reproducible.

\section{Discussion}
The original-generator findings below remain scoped to G1. The transfer discussion follows separately.
\input{tables/discussion_results.tex}
More frequent observations can increase short-event coverage and allow a debounced detector to confirm events before they disappear. They also expose more sensor artifacts, and the health detector itself receives a different sequence. Consequently, acquisition counts and fault-induced false reports must accompany Recall. A sampled raw health flag can be wrong or delayed; it is not evidence of a uniquely identified physical failure.

Emission U changes a different objective: it can preserve sampled event and health edges while reducing ordinary snapshots, thereby increasing normal-data age. Its diagnostic use on independent samplers is important: any similar gain there is evidence for a reusable communication rule, not exclusive novelty of the TAIS schedule. Joint sampling and communication effects must not be inferred by deleting health fields or comparing binary bytes against JSON on a competitor.

The historical H failure warns against interpreting a trend as a physical fault: slow environmental changes and stable offsets can resemble sensor drift, and added suspicion can suppress true events. Historical SU20 prioritizes detection with extra sampling; SU40 is a different resource operating point, not a universal improvement. The present comparison retains both, as well as GateOnly and the simpler GateOnly+U option. The study offers evidence for choosing a mode under explicit objectives, not a theorem that all objectives can be optimized simultaneously.

\section{Limitations and Threats to Validity}
\paragraph{Observability and identifiability.}
An event entirely between samples is unavailable to every sampled-only method. A fault that completely overwrites the true same-channel signal removes recoverable information. Without independent references, stable offsets and some drift/environmental-change combinations are ambiguous. More samples cannot reconstruct information that is absent from the sensor output. Greater Recall can coexist with greater fault-induced false reporting.

\paragraph{Synthetic scope and tuning.}
Outcomes come from two independently specified synthetic mechanisms, both designed within this project. G1 retains fixed profile families, temperature-only faults and narrow fault-relative timing. G2 adds stochastic background/noise, variable physical durations, independent fault/event schedules and both-channel faults; it is not an independently collected deployment dataset. Seeds are paired process replicates, not farms or sensor technologies. Event truth geometry differs: G1 uses interior amplitude labels, G2 full exogenous support. G2 is a zero-retuning stress test, not proof that its distribution is representative of agriculture. Rao-inspired retains public 24$^\circ$C/45\%RH priors, exposing prior mismatch rather than receiving a private G2 recalibration. The two-stage threshold and interval-bound grid is not an exhaustive search for each independent algorithm. Development threshold and budget searches introduce selection within that family; uncertainty intervals do not account for model misspecification. The extra bound search was disclosed during development, before any held-out test, and does not establish general optimality. No natural-fault labels or agricultural field events were evaluated. Previously obtained greenhouse files are not used to invent a source time axis.

\paragraph{Baseline and implementation scope.}
Delta and Variance are original lightweight engineering controls, not state-of-the-art literature reproductions. Rao-inspired omits major components of Rao's method. Shared ChangeAnalyzer limits detector diversity, and public-component variants must not be confused with external original methods. A faithful suitable literature reproduction remains absent. The completed second independently specified mechanism broadens synthetic coverage but does not substitute for that comparison or for independently collected natural-fault evidence. The current systems-study framing remains narrower than an algorithm-innovation claim.

\paragraph{Transport, hardware, and metrics.}
The main link is ideal local byte delivery. Application bytes exclude radio headers, receive windows, contention, spreading factor effects, loss and retries. LoRa airtime is modulation- and packet-dependent; neither byte ratios nor the additive sensitivity model measure actual wireless energy. There is no MCU timing, RAM characterization, current measurement or battery-lifetime validation. A sample cycle is a software processing opportunity, not measured sensor power gating. Conditional delays exclude misses; fault-induced attribution depends on the selected counterfactual coincidence window. The label matcher, pulse shapes and generator-specific channel arrangements may favor some event geometries. Multiple contrasts remain descriptive, and no global field effectiveness is established.
