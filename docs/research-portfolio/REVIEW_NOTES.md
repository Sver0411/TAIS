## Important chronology and interpretation corrections

1. Old v0.1/v0.2 reports record greenhouse-download failures at their execution dates. C0 subsequently acquired Hull; C1 still blocked its formal use for time semantics, and C1.1 acquired Greenhouse-1 under CC BY4.0. The portfolio states this sequence instead of copying “not acquired” as the current status or editing old documents.
2. The v0.1 same-channel 127→109 temporal matches and v0.2 140→146 result use different final seeds/denominators. They are never plotted as a single improving longitudinal score. OSP's617760rows are dependent combinations, not independent trials.
3. Confidence intervals are paired whole-seed or date-cluster intervals from existing CSVs. Total costs are not mislabeled as the mean per-seed differences stored in paired tables. Figures show point estimates, not fabricated marginal error bars. Zero-containing intervals do not certify equality or non-inferiority.
4. Figure5 intentionally has no green true-event band because its specified offset-only failure contains no event. Figure6 also shows late recovery-related reports, rather than hiding them to make the repaired trace look perfect. Its score axis preserves the large spike with a labeled symmetric-log scale.
5. Japanese FigureA/B reuse full Figure2/7; the English report has consistently numbered Figures1–8. The two schematics are labeled as such; there is no invented cloud control, deployed spatial module, or actuator fault label.
6. The current literature check could read arXiv2607.15235v1 with author-reported ISSC acceptance; no proceedings DOI was verified. Publisher/DOI browser requests for Rao/Masoum/CLARA encountered429 or tool-access errors in this pass. Existing project publisher-metadata/abstract evidence remains the stated basis, with Masoum full-text and CLARA complete-method limitations disclosed. ## Supervisor-facing assessment

The materials now make a concrete research discussion possible: a working causal prototype, negative and conditional results, an implementable failure diagnosis, and a falsifiable next information question. They do **not** yet make a deployment paper or broad algorithm-superiority claim supportable. The next study needs trustworthy availability/identity, eligible held-out coverage, local-event constraints grounded in the application, and evidence that true reference information beats an equal-cost uninformed control. The existing used data cannot be relabeled as an untouched validation set for that design.
