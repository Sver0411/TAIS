# Data provenance and validation scope

The final effectiveness data are entirely synthetic. There is no external real-data effectiveness experiment in this paper. Existing greenhouse intake files and unresolved clock semantics remain unchanged and are not interpolated, shifted, or re-labeled. The earlier Hull dataset DOI10.17632/54htxm94bv.1 is historical background only, not the source of these paper metrics.

Generator: frozen `src/tais/signals.py`; parameters: unchanged `configs/experiment.yaml`. Each1800s trace separates latent, pre-fault noisy and faulted streams. Gaussian noise sigma0.08°C/0.4%RH; smooth background amplitudes0.03°C/0.1%RH. Environmental trapezoids have4°C/16%RH amplitudes,5s ramps,26s/180s injected short/long support. Ground-truth labels use the frozen physical-unit label function, so the labeled window may differ from pulse support. Event onset follows the jittered fault schedule by60s. Faults: range, one-second spike, stuck, drift, missing, invalid and stable offset. Temperature faults with same-channel, humidity cross-channel or no event, two profiles, plus three clean cases give45 scenarios.

Audited development600–609 and final610–629 have no collisions in accessible historical structured records or archive identities. Full audit scope/limitations: `seed-audit.json`. Fresh noise/jitter seeds from one generator are not new real environments. Scenarios sharing one seed are correlated; paired bootstrap clusters by seed.

Source SHA, observed-stream SHA, signals/result/clean-counterfactual archive SHA are preserved per run and in manifests. Full original signals, sampled decisions, actual frame bytes/decoded receiver history are stored under ignored `results/traces/tais-paper/`. They are deterministically regenerated by `scripts/reproduce_paper.py`; committed CSVs and hashes support audit. Every transmitted frame was actually encoded, roundtrip checked and independently decoded during execution; the archive verifier checks file hashes without claiming a new physical-network experiment.

Version counts:53 native method/metadata/upload variants and29 shared-detector schedule views; these are not82 distinct independent algorithms. Each final method/view row aggregates20×45 runs and600 physical labels. No-event Recall and health-unavailable diagnostic metrics remain undefined. The root historical result directories and upstream submodules remain unchanged.

Auxiliary-field caution: per-run `payload_bytes` is a frozen inherited legacy counter (zero or pre-decoration JSON on added adapters), excluded from every paper cost summary/figure/claim. Only recomputed `binary_v1_bytes` is comparable communication evidence. Similarly `age_p95_s` in grouped summaries is the mean of per-run P95s; the paper uses `pooled_age_p95_s` from the actual pooled source-second distribution for overall age claims. Neither auxiliary field is silently substituted for its primary counterpart.
